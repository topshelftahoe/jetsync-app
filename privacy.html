Privacy Policy — JetSync: Travel & Recovery

Last updated: August 26, 2026

The short version

JetSync does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs. Everything stays on your device.

What data JetSync uses

On-device only. All protocol generation happens locally on your iPhone. JetSync uses:

  • Your inputs (chronotype, timezones, departure times, sleep hours): stored in your device's local memory and used only to generate your protocol. Never transmitted anywhere.
  • Notification scheduling: JetSync schedules local notifications on your device. These never leave your device and are not connected to any server.
  • Apple Watch sync: If you have a paired Apple Watch, your active protocol state is shared between your iPhone and Watch via Apple's WatchConnectivity framework, which is a direct device-to-device connection. No data passes through JetSync's servers (JetSync has no servers).
  • Live Activities: Your active protocol event is displayed in the Dynamic Island and on your Lock Screen using Apple's ActivityKit framework. This data stays on your device.

What JetSync does NOT do

  • JetSync does not collect analytics or usage data.
  • JetSync does not have user accounts or require sign-in.
  • JetSync does not use advertising or third-party tracking SDKs.
  • JetSync does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or health data.
  • JetSync does not transmit any data to any server.

Third-party services

None. JetSync uses only Apple system frameworks (WatchConnectivity, UserNotifications, ActivityKit). No third-party SDKs are included.

Children's privacy

JetSync does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date. Given that JetSync collects no data, changes are unlikely.
